SwimPace

Two timed swims - 400 meters and 200 meters - tell you your critical swim speed. SwimPace turns that one number into training zones and a workout with real send-off times you can take to the pool.

One test, one number

The 400/200 time trial is the standard CSS test. Your pace over the 200-meter difference is your threshold pace - the anchor for everything else.

Four honest zones

Recovery, aerobic, threshold, sprint - each a seconds-per-100 range around your CSS, updated the day you retest.

A workout, not a spreadsheet

Pick a focus and get warmup, main set and cooldown with per-rep targets and send-offs rounded to the clock you can actually read mid-pool.
